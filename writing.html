Working Papers

Public finance · Fiscal policy · Philippine economy

Fiscal Sustainability in the Philippines

An institutional assessment of competing interpretations of post-pandemic public debt, fiscal deficits, revenue capacity, and development-oriented fiscal space in the Philippines.

The paper compares government, multilateral, legislative, and academic perspectives while distinguishing short-term liquidity concerns from longer-term questions of solvency, fiscal capacity, and economic development.

Research in Progress

Monetary policy · Government securities · Fixed-income markets

Government Securities and the BSP Interest Rate Corridor

A developing study of the role of peso-denominated government securities within the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas’ interest-rate corridor from 2016 onward.

The project focuses on liquidity management, interest-rate transmission, collateral and market operations, and the broader development of the Philippine domestic government-securities market.
