MODEL//DISCLOSURE

Methodology and Interpretation

TRANSPARENT RULES

Bid-to-cover ratio

Tenders received divided by the announced amount offered. It measures submitted demand relative to the government’s planned issuance volume.

Award-to-offer ratio

Amount awarded divided by amount offered. It measures how much of the planned auction volume was accepted.

Bid acceptance ratio

Amount awarded divided by tenders received. It measures the share of submitted bids that was ultimately accepted.

Yield movement

The accepted yield is compared with the previous recorded auction for the same tenor and expressed in basis points.

Demand-signal thresholds
Below 1.00× Undersubscribed
1.00×–1.24× Thin demand
1.25×–1.99× Moderate demand
2.00× and above Strong demand
Limitations

Auction demand indicators do not independently measure investor sentiment, market liquidity, policy expectations, or debt sustainability. Results depend on the completeness and consistency of the uploaded dataset.
