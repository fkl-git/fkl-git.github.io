Research in Progress

Government Securities and the BSP Interest Rate Corridor

A developing study of how peso-denominated government securities function within the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas’ interest-rate corridor framework.

The project examines their role in liquidity management, interest-rate transmission, collateral and market operations, and the development of the domestic fixed-income market from 2016 onward.

Analytical Tools

Interactive Public-Finance Model

FISCAL//COMMAND: Debt Dynamics Lab

A browser-based sovereign debt modelling tool that projects debt-to-GDP trajectories under baseline, favorable, and adverse fiscal scenarios.

The model evaluates the interaction of nominal growth, effective interest rates, primary balances, stock-flow adjustments, and foreign-currency exposure. It calculates the debt-stabilizing primary balance, the balance required to reach a selected debt target, projected interest burdens, and the main drivers of the resulting debt path.

Scenarios can be saved locally, compared across assumptions, exported as CSV files, and printed as reports. The tool runs entirely in the browser and requires no external API, account, or paid service.
